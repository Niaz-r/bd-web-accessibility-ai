#001

Text color contrast requires review

color-contrast  •  UF-00001
High axe Color contrast WCAG 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) Level AA Axe automated finding
View issue details and remediation

Why this matters

Insufficient contrast between text and its background can make content difficult to read for users with low vision or color-vision deficiencies.

Location

DOM selector: .list:nth-child(1) > .hover\:text-primary.text-secondary.item:nth-child(1) > a[href$="topnews/2"] | Ownership: page_content | Actionability: directly_reviewable

Affected HTML 

<a href="/topnews/2">Payment through bkash for Admission form</a>

axe automated evidence 

Fix any of the following:
  Element has insufficient color contrast of 1.75 (foreground color: #ffb606, background color: #ffffff, font size: 12.0pt (16px), font weight: normal). Expected contrast ratio of 4.5:1

axe description

Ensure the contrast between foreground and background colors meets WCAG 2 AA minimum contrast ratio thresholds

Recommended change

Check the computed foreground and background colors of the affected text. Normal-sized text should generally have a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1, while qualifying large text should generally have a ratio of at least 3:1.

Manual review

Required: Yes

Why this matters

Insufficient contrast between text and its background can make content difficult to read for users with low vision or color-vision deficiencies.

Location

DOM selector: .bg-white\/5.sm\:p-8.border-white\/10:nth-child(3) > .items-start.sm\:mb-6.mb-4 > .mt-0\.5.group-hover\:text-blue-400 | Ownership: page_content | Actionability: directly_reviewable

Affected HTML

axe automated evidence 

Fix any of the following:
  Element's background color could not be determined because element contains an image node

axe description

Ensure the contrast between foreground and background colors meets WCAG 2 AA minimum contrast ratio thresholds

Recommended change

Check the computed foreground and background colors of the affected text. Normal-sized text should generally have a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1, while qualifying large text should generally have a ratio of at least 3:1.
