======================================================================
INTERPRETATION CAUTIONS
======================================================================
1. confirmed_by_both means the fusion algorithm matched a compatible custom and axe finding at the element level.
2. custom_only does not automatically mean axe missed a WCAG failure; some custom rules are contextual or best-practice indicators.
3. axe_only does not automatically mean the custom scanner failed; axe analyzes the rendered DOM and covers many rules outside the custom scanner's scope.
4. axe_incomplete findings require review and should not be counted as confirmed violations.
5. Context-aware recommendations are deterministic decision-support outputs and require human/developer verification before implementation.
