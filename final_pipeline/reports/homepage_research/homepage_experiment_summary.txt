Methodology note:
This exporter includes only pages belonging to the 80-homepage experimental sampling frame and only completed pipeline outputs. Earlier development and manual test pages are excluded.

The homepage experiment is separate from the frozen multi-page historical dataset and must not be used to recompute the original study statistics.

Homepage-level findings should not be interpreted as representing complete website-wide WCAG conformance.

axe incomplete findings are retained separately and are not treated as confirmed violations.

Context confidence represents strength of extracted contextual evidence rather than WCAG conformance certainty.
